CARTOGRAPHER VR R44 - BINDINGS AND RELOAD GUIDE

MENUS & COMFORT
- Point with either controller; press that hand's trigger to select.
- Right stick moves the menu cursor. A selects; B closes VR settings.
- Left stick navigates Halo's menus. Use Previous / Next for more Guide pages.
- Gameplay: X + RT pauses / resumes. Left-handed mode: A + LT. Release both afterward.
- Hold Y to recenter height and facing. In left-handed gameplay, hold B; menus still use Y.
- Comfort includes snap turning and world scale. HUD & Menus controls panel size and depth.
- Save & apply keeps changes. Reset to default resets ALL settings to the project baseline.

ON FOOT / RIGHT-HANDED MODE
- Left stick: move. Right stick: turn. A: jump. Left stick click: crouch.
- B: interact / action; hold B to enter or leave a vehicle or turret when prompted.
- Grip at a holster to draw; keep gripping to hold. Either hand can hold a weapon.
- Weapon-hand trigger: fire. Other trigger: zoom. With two guns, each trigger fires its gun.
- Right stick click: change grenade type. Physical grenades use their own chest slots.
- Move either hand beside the left side of your head and press grip to use the flashlight.
- Physical Melee Off: X melees. Physical Grenades Off: tap Y throws (unless used for reload).

LEFT-HANDED MODE
- Select Weapons > Left-handed controls and holsters, then Save & apply.
- Right stick: move / click to crouch. Left stick: turn / click to change grenades. X: jump.
- Y: interact / action. A + LT: pause. Hold B: recenter during gameplay.
- Weapon-hand trigger fires; the other trigger zooms. Two guns use their own triggers.
- Weapon holsters move to the left hip and shoulder. Flashlight gesture moves to the right.
- Manual reload buttons follow the actual gun hand: B for a right-hand gun, Y for a left-hand gun.
- Button fallbacks: A melees; tap B throws. Chest grenade types stay frag right / plasma left.

WEAPONS, HOLSTERS & DUAL WIELDING
- Either free hand can draw from either weapon holster. A grey mesh means that gun is in use.
- Release grip at a weapon holster to stow; release elsewhere to drop the gun.
- To replace a holstered weapon, first drop one; then grip when the ground pickup prompt appears.
- For dual wielding, briefly hold the free hand's grip at a compatible weapon pickup prompt.
- Either dual weapon can be dropped or holstered independently. Free a hand before manual reloads.
- Grip the support area with the free hand to two-hand. Some weapons keep one-hand aiming.
- You may release the trigger hand while the support hand holds; regrip the handle to resume.

PHYSICAL GRENADES & MELEE
- Chest holsters: frag on your right, plasma on your left. Grey means no grenades available.
- With a free hand, grip a chest grenade and keep holding grip while moving your hand.
- Release away from the chest to throw. Return to its chest slot and release to put it back.
- Swing the weapon hand to melee. Aim the melee with your head. Default threshold: 3.0 m/s.
- Energy Sword: swing for normal melee; hold its hand's trigger, then swing for a trigger attack.
- Weapons settings adjust grenade strength / grip height and melee speed, or disable either gesture.
- Native melee recovery still applies. Lowering the speed threshold does not remove that recovery.

MANUAL RELOAD / MAGAZINES
- Keep holding the gun's grip and free the other hand. Holster the second gun if dual wielding.
- Tap B for a gun in your right hand, or Y for a gun in your left, to eject its magazine.
- Grip at the labelled Magazine Zone opposite your weapon holsters; keep gripping the new magazine.
- Bring it to the weapon's magazine opening. BR, Magnum and Sniper feed below; SMG at the side.
- Carbine feeds from the top. Release grip after insertion, before grabbing a charging handle.
- Tactical reloads need no charging. BR / Magnum / SMG / Sniper need charging after emptying.
- Audio & Feedback > Reload sound volume: 0 mutes, 100 is normal, 200 is the maximum.

MANUAL RELOAD / CHARGING & SHOTGUN
- After an empty BR or Magnum reload, grip the rear-held handle / slide with your free hand.
- Guide it forward or release grip to let it return. Your hand snaps to the moving part.
- SMG / Sniper: grip the charging handle, pull it fully back, then return it or release grip.
- Shotgun: grip a shell at the Magazine Zone and bring it to the top loading port.
- Release grip and repeat for each shell. No magazine-eject button is needed.
- After each shot, keep your support grip held and move the pump back, then forward.
- You do not need to release and regrip between shots. An empty shotgun also needs a pump after loading.

MANUAL RELOAD / HEAVY & ALIEN WEAPONS
- Rocket Launcher: tap the gun-hand reload button (B right / Y left) to open the launcher.
- Grip each spent round at the opening and release to discard it before adding a new round.
- Grip fresh rockets at the Magazine Zone, insert one at a time, then tap the reload button to close.
- Brute Shot: tap reload to open, grip fresh ammo at the Magazine Zone and insert at the rear port.
- Then grip and pull the Brute Shot charging handle; return or release it to complete the reload.
- Fuel Rod: grip fresh ammo at the Magazine Zone and insert at its rear housing.
- Needler keeps its button reload. Battery weapons and the Energy Sword have no magazine reload.

VEHICLES, CUTSCENES & DIAGNOSTICS
- Hold the action button at the entry / exit prompt: B in right mode, Y in left mode.
- Warthog driver: RT accelerates, LT reverses; left stick steers and right stick gives free look.
- Vehicles settings offer third-person view and separate first-person height / offset adjustments.
- While boarding an enemy vehicle, swing the right hand to melee; swing the left to plant a grenade.
- Cutscenes remain in VR: look around, use the turn stick, or hold recenter to restore shot orientation.
- F6 toggles VR, F7 recenters, F8 captures diagnostics. Use Run-R3.cmd to collect the report ZIP (developers only).
- After losing focus or opening SteamVR, release all buttons, grips and sticks before resuming.
